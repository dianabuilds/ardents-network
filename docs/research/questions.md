# Research queue and decision handoff

Research is temporary decision work. Once a question is decided, its current
contract is promoted to an ADR, technical documentation, or both. Decided
records remain decision provenance after promotion. This table is a status
index, not a second specification; only records marked active or open are
research work. Delivery-horizon labels in record titles identify planning
history only.

Product scope determines whether an active question authorizes implementation.
An active record must state a falsifiable hypothesis, inputs, evidence,
limitations, and the decision it can unlock.
