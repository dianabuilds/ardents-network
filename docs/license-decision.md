# License Decision

Ardents Network source and project-authored release artifacts are licensed
under the MIT License. The canonical license text is the root `LICENSE` file.

The decision favors unrestricted use, modification, redistribution,
sublicensing, and commercial adoption while retaining the MIT copyright and
permission notice. Third-party code remains under its own license; bundled or
vendored notices must be preserved in source and binary distributions.

This decision does not make future AGPL dependencies automatically compatible
with Ardents' distribution shape. Any direct AGPL component, including a future
Katzenpost packet-engine candidate, requires a separate architecture and legal
decision or process separation that respects its license.
